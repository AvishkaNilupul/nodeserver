$100 is not seed capital for a real business. It is float — a bankroll big enough to unlock offers whose true value is 5× to 50× the deposit. The whole trick at this stage is to stop thinking about $100 as capital-to-invest and start thinking about it as a key that opens signup bonuses, welcome credits, wagering-requirement clears, and small-notional arbs that the professionals ignore because their opportunity cost is too high.

At $100, three things are true. First, almost every promo, welcome bonus, and matched deposit in existence is fully claimable — bonus caps are typically $10 to $2,500, right in your zone. Second, KYC is trivially cheap: one government ID, one selfie, one utility bill, and the same identity clears every venue. Third, the math is actually positive-EV before you place a single bet, because bookmakers and exchanges are paying you cash to become a user. Every dollar you deposit and clear is worth roughly $1.15–$1.90 back, guaranteed, across the plays below.

Realistic outcome band, one operator running all plays with normal week-nights and weekends: $100 → $250 by week 4, $500 by week 8, $1,500 by week 16, $4k–$6k by week 26. That's ~4,000–6,000% in six months, and none of it requires picking a market direction or getting lucky. It requires filling out forms, running one Node script per platform, and not fat-fingering a hedge.

The plays

1. Matched betting on sportsbook signup bonuses

Mechanics. Sportsbooks pay huge signup bonuses ($200 free bet on a $25 qualifying wager is standard 2026 US pricing at DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, ESPN Bet, Fanatics, bet365, Hard Rock Bet, Bally Bet). You place the qualifying bet at the sportsbook and simultaneously lay the opposite outcome at Betfair Exchange (UK/EU), Smarkets, ProphetX or Sporttrade (US). Whichever way the game goes, one side wins and the other loses within your calculated margin. The free bet, once triggered, is then converted to ~70–80% of face value using the same lay technique.

Real math, one cycle. DraftKings 2026 promo: bet $5, get $200 in bonus bets on qualification.

  • Qualifying bet: $5 on Lakers ML @ +100 at DraftKings. Lay Lakers @ 2.02 at ProphetX for $4.95. Cost: ~$0.10 regardless of outcome.
  • $200 bonus bet fires. Place on a high-odds market, say Team X @ +400 (5.00). Lay @ 5.10 at ProphetX with $154.
  • Bonus wins: +$800 book profit, -$630 exchange loss = +$170.
  • Bonus loses: $0 book, +$154 exchange win, minus staked $154 lay balance = still ~+$154 (bonus doesn't return stake, so you extract via lay).
  • Guaranteed profit: ~$150 net on a single US sportsbook.

Rare loss scenarios: lay odds move against you between placing the book bet and the exchange bet (execution slippage, ~$3–8 per cycle worst case), or you get gubbed (bookmaker restricts your account) before extracting the bonus (rare in first 30 days).

2. Casino signup bonus laddering

Mechanics. Casino deposit bonuses come with wagering requirements ("1× the bonus" is best, 10–15× is standard, 40× is a trap). You deposit, receive bonus funds, and grind through the WR on the lowest-variance, highest-RTP slot the casino allows. Expected loss is deposit × WR × (1 - RTP). If bonus EV exceeds expected grind loss, it's positive.

4. Hyperliquid / Aevo / Drift funding-rate arbitrage

Mechanics. Perp funding is paid every 1h (Hyperliquid), 8h (most CEXes), or continuously (Aevo). When funding rate on a perp is heavily positive, shorts receive funding from longs. If you short the perp on the venue with hot funding and buy the identical spot elsewhere, you are delta-neutral and collect funding on your notional every period.

Real 2025 example (this pattern recurs monthly). In July 2025, HYPE-PERP on Hyperliquid ran at +0.15% per hour funding for ~40 hours during a listing squeeze — that's 400%+ annualized. Play: short 5× HYPE-PERP on HL with $100 margin (=$500 notional), buy $500 spot HYPE on Bybit or Binance. Funding collected: $500 × 0.0015/hr × 40hr = $30 in 40 hours on a $100 base. Delta = 0.

Where to find hot funding. Coinglass (coinglass.com/funding-rate/exchange), or roll your own via each exchange's public funding endpoint. Anything above +50% annualized (that's ~0.014%/8h) on a coin that also trades spot on a major CEX is a candidate.

Entry sizing. With $100, use 3–5× leverage on the perp side to get $300–500 notional, matched with spot. Keep 30% cushion for margin swings — funding rate opportunities often occur when the coin is pumping, so your short can be underwater on P&L even though it's hedged by the spot.

Variance profile. Low if executed cleanly. Killers: (a) funding flips negative before you close the trade; (b) one leg gets liquidated because you underestimated margin needs; (c) borrow-to-short is unavailable on the spot leg for exotic tokens. Stick to top 30 coins by volume, exit when funding drops below your hurdle (+15% APR).

5. Kalshi vs Polymarket vs sportsbook cross-arbitrage

Mechanics. The same underlying event (Fed rate decision, Bitcoin price, election, Super Bowl) is priced on three regulated venues with different user bases and different implied probabilities. Buy YES at the cheap venue, NO at the expensive one, guaranteed profit when the sum of prices is < $1.00.

Real numbers. 2024 election contracts spent weeks with 5–8% gaps between Kalshi and Polymarket. 2026 crypto price contracts (BTC > $150k end of year) still show 3–4% gaps regularly because of demographic split — Polymarket skews crypto-native optimistic, Kalshi skews TradFi conservative. Fed decision contracts routinely show 2% gaps in the 48 hours before FOMC meetings.

API access.

  • Kalshi REST: https://demo-api.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2/ (demo), production is trading-api.kalshi.com, docs at kalshi.com/docs. Free, key-based, no fees on maker orders.
  • Polymarket: CLOB API at https://clob.polymarket.com, plus subgraph at gamma-api.polymarket.com. Gasless on Polygon.
  • Sporttrade / ProphetX: private APIs, but public order books; scrape or use community Python wrappers.

Entry sizing. With $100 split 50/50 across Kalshi and Polymarket (fund Polymarket via USDC on Polygon, ~$0.05 gas per trade), you can hold ~5 simultaneous positions of $20 each. Cycle winnings weekly.

Variance profile. Zero when both sides fill at posted prices. Killer is one leg not filling (liquidity gap on Kalshi's thinner books) — always place the less liquid side first as a maker, and only then hit the more liquid book as a taker.

7. Thrift-to-eBay / Depop retail arbitrage

Mechanics. Mispriced physical items exist at every thrift store, estate sale, and Facebook Marketplace listing. Scanning apps + comps give you an instant "is this worth $X" answer. You buy, ship, list, collect.

Tools.

  • SoldQuick (iOS/Android, free tier): scan a barcode or photo, get eBay Sold comps within 3 seconds. Paid tier ($15/mo) adds sell-through rate.
  • Book Scouter (books specifically): scans ISBN, quotes 30+ book buyback vendors, one-tap sell.
  • Amazon Seller App (Camera → barcode): instant FBA fee and current price. Best for books, video games, sealed collectibles.
  • Flippi / TeraPeak (eBay's own comp tool, $19.99/mo for Basic Store subscribers): full sold history 3 years back.

Entry sizing with $100. Two Saturdays at Goodwill/Salvation Army with a scanner: aim for 15–25 items in the $3–$8 range that comp at $25–$80 sold on eBay. Typical margin per successful item: $18 net after eBay fees + shipping. Hit rate: 1 in 6 scans at a well-picked store. So a $100 buy nets ~$400 gross, ~$280 net after fees and shipping in 3–4 weeks.

Best 2026 categories. Vintage Pyrex, Le Creuset, coach purses (yes still), Sonos speakers, Nintendo/Sega retro, video-game consoles (any), Lego sets (sealed = jackpot, opened = scan for rare minifigs), Y2K clothing (Depop), designer denim (Depop).

Variance profile. Medium. You will buy losers. Rule: never spend more than $10 without a scan confirming ≥3× comp. 30-day holding period average, some items sit 90 days.

Real code where useful

Funding rate scanner (drop in services/fundingScanner.js)

const ccxt = require('ccxt');
const { Hyperliquid } = require('hyperliquid');

const HURDLE_APR = 0.25; // only alert on 25%+ annualized
const VENUES = ['bybit', 'binanceusdm', 'okx', 'bitget'];

async function scan() {
  const hl = new Hyperliquid();
  const hlMeta = await hl.info.perpsMeta();
  const hlFunding = await hl.info.metaAndAssetCtxs();

  const opps = [];
  for (let i = 0; i < hlMeta.universe.length; i++) {
    const coin = hlMeta.universe[i].name;
    const fundingHourly = parseFloat(hlFunding[1][i].funding);
    const apr = fundingHourly * 24 * 365;
    if (Math.abs(apr) < HURDLE_APR) continue;

    // find matching spot on a CEX
    for (const venueName of VENUES) {
      const venue = new ccxt[venueName]();
      const symbol = `${coin}/USDT`;
      try {
        const ticker = await venue.fetchTicker(symbol);
        opps.push({
          coin, hl_apr: apr, spot_venue: venueName,
          spot_price: ticker.last,
          direction: apr > 0 ? 'short_HL_long_spot' : 'long_HL_short_spot',
        });
        break;
      } catch (_) { /* no spot on this venue */ }
    }
  }

  return opps.sort((a, b) => Math.abs(b.hl_apr) - Math.abs(a.hl_apr));
}

module.exports = { scan };

Run under PM2 on a 60-second cron. Push to a Mongo funding_opps collection, alert via Telegram when APR > 100%.

Kalshi ↔ Polymarket arb detector

const axios = require('axios');
const { ClobClient } = require('@polymarket/clob-client');

const KALSHI_BASE = 'https://trading-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2';
const MIN_EDGE = 0.03; // 3 cents

async function findArbs(mappings /* [{kalshi_ticker, poly_market_id, ...}] */) {
  const results = [];
  const poly = new ClobClient('https://clob.polymarket.com', 137);

  for (const m of mappings) {
    const [k, p] = await Promise.all([
      axios.get(`${KALSHI_BASE}/markets/${m.kalshi_ticker}`),
      poly.getOrderBook(m.poly_market_id),
    ]);

    const kalshiYesAsk = k.data.market.yes_ask / 100; // cents to $
    const polyNoBid = parseFloat(p.bids[0].price); // NO bid on poly

    // Buy YES on Kalshi + NO on Polymarket ~= 1.00 payout
    const cost = kalshiYesAsk + (1 - polyNoBid);
    if (cost < 1 - MIN_EDGE) {
      results.push({
        event: m.name, cost, edge: 1 - cost,
        buy_kalshi_yes_at: kalshiYesAsk,
        buy_poly_no_at: (1 - polyNoBid),
      });
    }
  }
  return results;
}

Retail arb: cache eBay sold comps by UPC (reduce SoldQuick API bill)

// mongo: db.upc_comps { _id: upc, median_sold: n, sample_size: n, ts: Date }
async function comp(upc) {
  const cached = await db.collection('upc_comps').findOne({ _id: upc });
  if (cached && Date.now() - cached.ts < 7*24*3600*1000) return cached;

  const sold = await ebay.finding.findCompletedItems({
    productId: { '@type': 'UPC', '#value': upc },
    itemFilter: [{ name: 'SoldItemsOnly', value: 'true' }],
    'paginationInput.entriesPerPage': 30,
  });
  const prices = sold.searchResult[0].item?.map(
    i => parseFloat(i.sellingStatus[0].currentPrice[0].__value__)) || [];
  const median = prices.sort((a,b)=>a-b)[Math.floor(prices.length/2)] || 0;

  const doc = { _id: upc, median_sold: median, sample_size: prices.length, ts: new Date() };
  await db.collection('upc_comps').replaceOne({_id: upc}, doc, {upsert: true});
  return doc;
}

Operational hazards

Matched betting: "gubbing" — sportsbooks silently limit accounts that only hit promos. Cure: mode-mix in some small "punt" bets (parlay a Sunday NFL card for $5, lose it), stay under $500 stakes for the first 3 months, do not withdraw immediately after every bonus clear. UK books gub faster than US in 2026.

Casino bonuses: bonus-abuse pattern detection. Never bet max stake while a bonus is active — most casinos cap at $5/spin during WR and will void winnings above that. Read the T&Cs for excluded games (slots-only is common). Withdraw to the same method you deposited with (KYC-compliance rule everywhere).

Crypto exchange stacking: ACH reversal windows. Chase, BoA, and a few credit unions will unwind ACH pulls up to 60 days if flagged — do not withdraw crypto you deposited via ACH until day 61. Also: some exchanges (Kraken specifically) require KYC re-verification if you close and reopen — one shot per identity.

Funding rate arb: cross-venue collateral is not portable. If your Hyperliquid short goes deep underwater P&L-wise while your Bybit spot is up, you need to manually move USDC between venues to top up HL margin, which takes 2–5 minutes and can be catastrophic in a fast wick. Preload 30% cushion on the perp leg.

Prediction market arb: Polymarket YES + Kalshi NO on "same event" is only an arb if both venues resolve identically. Read both rulebooks — 2024 election contracts had cases where Polymarket resolved on AP call and Kalshi on Congress certification, days apart. Mismatch = you might be right on one and lose on the other for weeks.

Region arb: platform bans. Discord will nuke a TR-Nitro account with 24h notice if payment method / VPN doesn't match — buy monthly, not yearly. Steam's VAC-like enforcement on region-shift is rare but permanent; use a dedicated Argentina profile that never touches your main.

Retail arb: eBay's "unpaid item" / "item not as described" scams. Video-record every high-value pack-out. Ship with signature required on anything over $75. Never accept returns on video games or trading cards (buyer switches your item for a beater).
