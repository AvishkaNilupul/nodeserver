Banned accounts

Accounts Twitch has deleted or suspended — confirmed with the public user query, not merely a rejected token. This is separate from token invalid, which only means the token expired and the account can be re-authed; the Drops Archive lumps the two together, which is why a ban wave is invisible there. Bans arrive in waves, so everything here is arranged on a time axis and against the population it came from.

One bar per day. Red bars are waves — days that took more than triple the daily average.
