Integrity guard

A background checker runs every 5 minutes over all live auto-delivery listings: it tops sold-down Plati / GGSel listings back up with fresh server accounts, and flags anything that could burn a buyer. Findings that have a safe one-click remedy show a Fix button — replace a burned account, re-reserve drops, detach a sold unit, or retry a restock — and everything else can be resolved or ignored by hand.
