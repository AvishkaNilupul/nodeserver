Twitch drop claim race

Paste a Twitch auth-token cookie, pick a claimable drop, and fire N parallel DropsPage_ClaimDropRewards mutations at once. Each worker lands with its own Client-Session-Id and X-Device-Id so Twitch's inventory service sees N distinct "devices" hitting the same dropInstanceID in the same instant. The token never leaves this server.

1 · Inventory

twitch.tv → F12 → Application → Cookies → auth-token.

2 · Fire

select a drop on the left to arm the race
1 – 10000. big fires cost time in warmup (own TCP+TLS per worker).
