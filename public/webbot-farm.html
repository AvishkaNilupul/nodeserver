Auto power start/stop bots on live streams · heartbeat from container logs
When on, each bot runs only while its game has a live campaign stream and is stopped (kept, not released) while the game is dark; uncertainty keeps bots on and a manual Stop stays stopped until you Start. The watcher also writes each bot's channels.json (campaign ACL + live ACL channels) and reads a per-bot heartbeat from its logs — a bot that is running but credits nothing while its game is live is flagged idle and alerted on Telegram.
