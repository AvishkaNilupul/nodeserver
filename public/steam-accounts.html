How accounts get here, and what to check before selling

How accounts get here

  1. Make accounts in the Steam generator on your Mac (127.0.0.1:4108).
  2. Each finished account uploads here by itself. If the server can't be reached, the Mac keeps it and tries again.
  3. Uploading again never overwrites anything you changed on this page.

Before you list an account

  • Add the games that are on it, with their store price.
  • Check the store region in Steam and set it here. The signup country is not proof of the store region.
  • Set the stage to On sale when it is listed, and Sold when it sells, so it is never sold twice.
